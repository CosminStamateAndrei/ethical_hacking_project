# Bunq Legal Judgement Assessment

The goal of this document is to provide guidelines to produce a verdict and a
written justification.

Verdict options:
- Ethical hacking / Computer intrusion;
- low / medium / high severity.

## Method

Apply the assessment flow:

1. CVD policy check;
2. Three conditions;
3. Severity.

### CVD policy check

Is there a policy, and is the conduct within scope?

### Three conditions (Y/N each)

1. Substantial societal interest;
2. proportionality;
3. subsidiarity.

- All Y → ethical hacking.
- Any N → computer intrusion.

### Severity:

Apply the matrix (YYN/YNY → low; YNN/NYY → medium; NYN/NNY/NNN → high),
noting additional factors: intent, harm, attempt vs. completion.

## Deliverable

Per case, the verdict plus the justification addressing the three conditions, CVD compliance, severity if applicable, and what the actor should do.
